# Nilafer’s Blue Water Lily Birthday Experience

## Experience
- Replace the blank page with a cinematic, mobile-first night garden in deep navy, sapphire, silver, and moonlit white.
- Open with a blooming-lily loading moment, immersive lake scene, birthday message, live countdown, music control, and scroll cue.
- Use Nilafer’s four supplied photos throughout the memories experience, with respectful crops and elegant framing.

## Story Sections
- Build “Why You’re So Special” as six glowing, expandable qualities around the featured quote.
- Add a masonry memory gallery with zooming lightbox, keyboard navigation, mobile swipe, and multi-image preview upload.
- Build an animated story timeline with editable sample memories and optional photo attachments.
- Create an interactive blue-lily garden where blooms open to reveal qualities and memories.
- Add an opening handwritten letter, twelve reveal-on-tap reasons, and a floating Polaroid wishes wall.
- Finish with a full-screen surprise transformation and a moonlit closing scene carrying the exact final messages.

## Motion and Sound
- Use restrained cinematic reveals, water ripples, drifting petals, glowing stars, and scroll-driven lily blooming.
- Add a gentle generated ambient soundscape with an accessible on/off control, avoiding autoplay restrictions.
- Respect reduced-motion settings and lazy-load noncritical photography.

## Technical Details
- Implement in the project’s existing React/TanStack framework, preserving its required routing rather than replacing it with Next.js.
- Use semantic design tokens and elegant serif/sans typography in the global design system.
- Keep customization data centralized near the page content so names, memories, reasons, wishes, and dates are easy to change.
- Validate the final result at desktop and mobile sizes, including gallery, letter, lilies, countdown, and surprise interactions.
